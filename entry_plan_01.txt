Plan: 30-day wealth building with $100

Strategy: Crypto arbitrage + momentum trading across exchanges.

Days 1-3: Setup
- Create Binance, KuCoin, Bybit wallets
- Setup API with $50 order limits, $25 risk per trade

Days 4-7: Market Analysis
- Pull hourly data, calculate returns and volatility
- Identify top 10% by volatility with good liquidity

Days 8-14: Arbitrage
- Buy low, sell high across exchanges (0.5% spread)
- $10 per trade, track fill rates (target 60%+)
- Reinvest 50% of profits

Days 15-21: Momentum
- Buy at 1.5% pullbacks if up >2% in 6h
- Target +3%, exit at -2% stop loss
- Switch to mean reversion if win rate <45%

Days 22-30: Risk Management
- Scale positions: 10% to 15% to 25% of account
- Daily drawdown: liquidate at -5% loss
- Watch correlations, daily strategy review

Goal: End 30 days with >$200 (100% return).
